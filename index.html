Mortgage Guidance

Can You Sell a Property With an Outstanding Home Loan?

Yes. A property with an outstanding home loan may be sold, subject to the lender's requirements and the applicable transaction process across Indian financial institutions. If you are considering selling a residential property while your home loan is still active, the first step is to understand your current outstanding loan principal, current local property market value, and loan closure documentation requirements.
